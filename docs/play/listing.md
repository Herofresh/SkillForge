## Full description (≤ 4000)

```
Turn calisthenics into an RPG. SkillForge maps bodyweight training as a skill tree: start with the basics, earn XP with every set, level up your exercises and unlock harder skills – from push-ups and pull-ups to handstands, levers, the planche, splits and acrobatics.

THE SKILL TREE
• 155 exercises in 14 branches: push, pull, legs, core, handstand, planche, front lever, back lever and rings, dynamic skills, flexibility, mobility and acrobatics
• Every exercise has a short description, form cues and a looping pixel animation
• Prove a skill with its Trial to unlock what comes next – or unlock it yourself: the app suggests, you decide
• Add your own exercises or change any progression to fit your training

WORKOUTS FOR YOUR TIME AND PLACE
• Pick your equipment (home, park, rings…) and a time from 15 to 90 minutes
• SkillForge builds a session for your goals: skill work, paired strength, core and a cool-down
• It learns your pace from the rests you actually take, so a 30-minute plan really takes about 30 minutes
• Double progression: hit every set and the next target goes up
• Built-in set timer, hold countdown and rest timer with vibration
• Swap, add or skip anything before and during the session

LEVEL UP YOUR HERO
• Character level, six attributes (push, pull, core, legs, balance, mobility) and ranks
• 15 hero classes with three tiers each, and a weekly class challenge
• A pixel companion that cheers you on, earns gear and changes its mood with your training
• Home-screen widgets for your streak, level and companion

TRAIN WITH CARE
• Tendon-friendly guidance for straight-arm skills: recommended training time before a Trial, a per-session volume budget and rest days – shown as advice, never as a lock
• Built-in guide that explains every system

YOUR DATA, ON YOUR PHONE
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything is stored on your phone; back it up to a file whenever you like
• Delete all your data with one tap

SkillForge gives general training suggestions, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before you start a new exercise programme, and stop if you feel pain.
```
